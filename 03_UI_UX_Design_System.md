# 1. Design Vision

The Code Realm should feel like entering a futuristic developer operating system.

The visitor is not simply browsing a website.

They are exploring the digital world of a software engineer.

The design combines:

- Premium developer portfolio.
- AAA game interface.
- Futuristic HUD.
- Cyberpunk atmosphere.
- Modern SaaS quality.
- Minimal professional design.

The final experience should communicate:

"This developer builds serious software, but thinks creatively."

---

# 2. Design Philosophy

The design follows one rule:

## "Immersive, not distracting."

The portfolio should feel like a game interface while maintaining professional usability.

Every visual decision must answer:

Does this improve understanding?

Does this improve storytelling?

Does this improve trust?

If the answer is no, remove it.

# 5. Design Principles

## Principle 1

Premium over flashy.

The portfolio should never look like a gaming fan page.

---

## Principle 2

Information hierarchy first.

Important information must always be visible.

---

## Principle 3

Motion should explain.

Animations should guide attention.

---

## Principle 4

Consistency everywhere.

Every card.

Every button.

Every panel.

Every interaction.

Must belong to the same universe.

---

## Principle 5

Professional readability.

Visitors should easily understand:

Who I am.

What I build.

Why my work matters.

# 22. Motion Guidelines

Animations should:

- Be under 500ms.
- Feel smooth.
- Support understanding.
- Respect reduced motion.

Avoid:

- Constant moving backgrounds.
- Excessive particles.
- Long cinematic delays.

## Tablet

# 24. Accessibility

Requirements:

Keyboard navigation.

High contrast.

Readable typography.

ARIA labels.

Reduced motion support.

---

# 25. Design Restrictions

Do not use:

❌ Cartoon graphics

❌ Pixel art

❌ Excessive neon

❌ Gaming fonts

❌ Distracting animations

❌ Complex 3D without purpose

❌ Low readability layouts
